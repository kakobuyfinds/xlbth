The buying guide

Four checks before you order

01. Verify USFans product links

Save the original seller listing and record the marketplace, item ID, size, and color. A Weidian product link, Taobao listing ID, or 1688 product page can help you match the same item if an agent link stops working. If two sellers use identical pictures, compare their size charts and available variants before assuming the items match.

02. Use a USFans sizing guide

For low-top sneakers and mesh running shoes, measure both feet and compare the longer foot length in centimeters with the seller size chart. If you have an insole measurement, check what the seller measured before comparing. For cotton jersey T-shirts, relaxed-fit hoodies, and lightweight jackets, measure flat chest width and shoulder width on a garment you already wear. Check waist and inseam measurements for pants and shorts, and compare football jersey sizing, matching tracksuits, and underwear size charts item by item.

03. Read USFans QC photos in context

Seller images show the listed style. Older buyer photos show a previous unit, and warehouse inspection photos may show your own order. Ask for a size label photo and the angles that matter to you, such as an outsole close-up, stitching details around ribbed cuffs, or a clasp closure on enamel bracelets. Compare the photos with your selected variant while the return window is open.

04. Build a USFans shipping estimate

Keep the item subtotal, currency conversion, agent fees, and freight separate. For shipping to the United States, estimate with your destination ZIP code, packed weight, and parcel dimensions. A bulky shoebox may affect volumetric weight on some routes. Check the carrier's billing method and what the quote includes before you approve dispatch.

FAQ

Questions before you order

What is a USFans spreadsheet?

It is a directory of shopping finds, often organized by category, with links to product listings or shopping-agent pages.

Where do the category links go?

They open HipobuyFind collections. This independent guide has no affiliation with USFans or the brands shown.

What if a product link is broken?

Look for the original marketplace and listing ID. If a replacement link opens, confirm the seller and the exact size and color before relying on it.

Do QC photos prove an item is authentic?

QC photos help you inspect visible condition and details. For authenticity, use a reputable seller with documented provenance or an independent authentication service.

How do I estimate shipping to the United States?

Use your destination ZIP code, packed weight, and parcel dimensions in the agent's estimator. Check what the quote includes and request an updated estimate once the parcel is packed.
